# niixtube

A browser extension (Chrome + Firefox) that changes how YouTube behaves, so you see what you actually want to see.

## What it does

- **Subscriptions-only home** — your home feed shows only videos from channels you're subscribed to, sorted newest first, instead of YouTube's algorithmic picks.
- **Watched videos are dimmed** — so it's easy to spot what's new at a glance, without them disappearing entirely.
- **Queue videos and let them play automatically** — add a video to the queue from its watch page, from a thumbnail, or by right-clicking any video link. Queued videos play one after another in the same tab.
- **Shorts are hidden** — everywhere: home, sidebar, and any Shorts link redirects to the normal video page.
- **No more "Video paused. Continue watching?"** — dismissed automatically.
- **Original audio, not auto-dubbed** — if a video has multiple audio tracks, the original one plays automatically instead of a dubbed version.
- **Original titles, not auto-translated** — shows the title exactly as the uploader wrote it.

Every one of these can be turned on or off individually in the extension's settings.
